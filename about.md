I'm a software engineer who recently completed a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in [Software Engineering](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-engineering-honours-2455/software-engineering-softwe2455) at the University of Queensland. I build multi-agent AI systems and LLM applications — most recently as founder and CTO of [Gödel](https://godel.sh), an agent platform built on LangGraph and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) that grew to A$100K ARR before I wound it down at the end of 2025.

I love programming and building hardware, and I currently tutor UQ's pattern recognition course while looking for my next full-time role.
